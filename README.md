# Provision

A week of food and training from the numbers a ring or watch already has.

You pick one want: steadier energy, strength, slow fat loss, clearer skin, a race, or better sleep. Then you type a day, paste it from Oura, Apple Watch, Garmin, or Whoop, or import a CSV. Provision picks an easy, steady, or hard day from sleep, HRV, and resting heart rate, and writes three plates to match.

The writing is a set of rules in `app.js`, not a chat model. Numbers stay in the browser. The phone gives a short tap when you check a plate, switch a want, or copy the list, on phones that allow it. Open it and the next thing is on the day itself, with how today compares to yesterday. A calendar on the same page keeps the habits you are practicing. If you ate out, count that meal and move on. Weight can be in kilograms or pounds. The shop list can be today or the whole week.

It does not diagnose or replace a clinician.

Open `index.html`, or the GitHub Pages site.
